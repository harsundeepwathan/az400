# Typical furniture sizes

Width × depth in millimetres, for sketching before anything is measured. Real
pieces vary by ±150 mm, so measure before you buy — and measure the route in
(front door, hall corner, stair turn) as well as the space it lands in.

## Seating

| Piece | Typical | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 2-seat sofa | 1600 × 900 | Seats two adults properly, not "two-and-a-half". |
| 3-seat sofa | 2100 × 950 | Corner units start at 2400 × 1600 overall. |
| Armchair | 800 × 850 | Add 100 mm each way for a recliner's footprint at rest. |
| Recliner, extended | 800 × 1600 | Check the reclined depth, not the upright one. |
| Ottoman / footstool | 600 × 450 | |
| Dining chair | 450 × 500 | 600 mm of table edge per person. |
| Bar stool | 400 × 400 | 300 mm between seat and counter underside. |

## Heights that matter

- Worktop 900, breakfast bar 1050, dining table 750, coffee table 400–450.
- Seat height 420–450; a sofa seat 550 deep suits sitting up, 650 suits lounging.
- Hanging rail: 1000 mm for shirts, 1800 mm for full-length coats and dresses.
- Art hung so its centre is 1500 mm above the floor; over a sofa, 150–250 mm
  above the back.
- A pendant over a dining table hangs 750–900 mm above the table top.
