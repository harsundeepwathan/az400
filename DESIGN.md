# Design System: Vector

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Grouped Standard"**

Vector plays the category standard straight. It is built the way Apple Health and Fitness, Strong, MacroFactor and Whoop are built: a grey grouped ground, white cells floating on it by tone alone, one green tint that means "you can act here", and a fixed colour per area so the header of a cell tells you where you are before you read it. Nothing is invented for its own sake. The craft is in doing the native thing exactly: continuous corners, Dynamic Type everywhere, big rounded numerals, sentence-case labels, and one decision on screen at a time.

The density is comfortable rather than packed. Today is a single column of cells separated by a 36 pt section rhythm on a 16 pt gutter; each cell opens with a tinted category header, states one thing in a bold title, backs it with numbers in SF Pro Rounded, and offers at most one filled action. Dark mode is designed, not inverted: a pure black ground with #1C1C1E cells and a brighter green that carries black text.

The system refuses the gamified (achievement badges, confetti, neon), the clinical (spreadsheet coldness) and the "bro" register (aggressive black-and-red). Recommendations come from rules, so they are marked with a scope, never a sparkle.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Grey grouped ground, white cells, no strokes or shadows on content.
- One green tint for actions, selection, links and the tab bar.
- Category colour lives in the cell's header: Coach and Training green, Nutrition orange, Body indigo.
- Macros are a fixed categorical set: protein blue, carbs teal, fat amber, calories orange.
- SF Pro for words, SF Pro Rounded bold with tabular figures for numbers.
- Sentence case everywhere; no eyebrows, no tracked uppercase labels.
- One filled primary per view; secondary actions are tinted, not filled.

## Colors

A neutral iOS grouped palette carrying one action tint and a small, fixed set of meaning colours; every token has a hand-picked dark value.

### Primary
- **Action Green** (`tint`, dark `tint-dark`): the only fill for primary buttons, the selected tab, the trained-day dots in the week strip and the Pro badge. Text on it is white in light mode and black on the brighter dark-mode green (`text-on-tint-dark`).
- **Action Green Text** (`tint-text`): the darker light-mode green used for links, trailing header actions ("Coach", "See the evidence"), secondary-button labels, icon-badge glyphs and the app-wide `.tint`. Also the Training category tint.
- **Green Wash** (`tint-soft`): the fill behind secondary buttons, icon badges and the avatar initial.

### Secondary
- **Category Orange** (`nutrition`): Nutrition's header tint. The calorie ring uses its sibling **Calorie Orange** (`calories`).
- **Category Indigo** (`body`): Body weight and measurements.

### Tertiary
- **Macro Blue / Teal / Amber** (`protein`, `carbs`, `fat`): a categorical set validated for colour-vision separation and always direct-labelled with a dot and the macro name.
- **Status** (`success`, `warning`, `danger`, with `warning-soft` and `danger-soft` washes): always shipped with an arrow, sign, icon or word. `success` shares the green text value; `warning` also colours PR badges and "kcal over".

### Neutral
- **Grouped Ground** (`grouped-ground`, dark `grouped-ground-dark`): the screen background behind everything.
- **Cell White** (`cell`, dark `cell-dark`): every content cell.
- **Well** (`well`, dark `well-dark`): fills inside a cell: chips, quiet buttons, quick-action tiles, evidence lists, ring and bar tracks.
- **Separator** (`separator`, dark `separator-dark`): 0.5 pt hairlines inside cells only.
- **Ink** (`ink`, white in dark), **Secondary Grey** (`text-secondary`), **Tertiary Grey** (`text-tertiary`): text hierarchy. Tertiary is reserved for chevrons, dismiss glyphs and disabled fills.
- **Chart Muted / Target Band** (`chart-muted`, `chart-target`): de-emphasised series and the soft green target band.

### Named Rules
**The One Tint Rule.** Green is the only colour that means "act". If something is tappable and filled, it is Action Green; nothing else gets a filled button.

**The Header Carries the Category Rule.** Category colour appears in a cell's `CategoryHeader` symbol and label (and in matching row icons), never as a cell fill, border or background wash.

**The Never Colour Alone Rule.** Status and macro colours are always paired with an icon, sign or label.

## Typography

**Body Font:** SF Pro (system), every style built on a Dynamic Type text style.
**Metric Font:** SF Pro Rounded, bold, tabular figures.

**Character:** One native family for words, its rounded sibling for numbers. The rounded bold numerals are the warmth of the system; the rest stays plain so the numbers lead.

### Hierarchy
- **Large Title** (700, 34 pt): the screen title ("Today") only.
- **Title** (700, 22 pt): the one statement a cell makes: workout name, coach headline.
- **Section Title** (700, 20 pt): `SectionHeader` titles between cells ("Daily insight", "This week").
- **Title 3** (600, 20 pt): exercise names, upgrade statements.
- **Headline** (600, 17 pt): sub-heads inside cells ("Your week"), empty-state titles.
- **Body / Body Emphasized** (400 / 600, 17 pt): content and button labels.
- **Secondary / Secondary Emphasized** (400 / 600, 15 pt): supporting text, category headers, inline actions, evidence rows.
- **Caption / Caption Emphasized** (500 / 600, 12 pt): metric labels, chips, macro ring titles.
- **Metric Hero** (Rounded 700, 34 pt, tabular): calories left, the calorie decision, workout clock.
- **Metric** (Rounded 700, 22 pt, tabular): card metrics and the recommendation headline.
- **Metric Small** (Rounded 700, 17 pt, tabular): ring centres and inline stats.
- **Data / Data Secondary** (600 / 400, tabular): set tables and row values.

### Named Rules
**The Sentence Case Rule.** Every label, header, button and title is sentence case. No uppercase, no letter-spacing, no small label above a heading.

**The Rounded Numbers Rule.** A number the user is meant to read as a result is SF Pro Rounded bold with tabular figures and animates with a numeric content transition. Words never use the rounded face.

**The Dynamic Type Rule.** Sizes above are the default Large setting; every style scales. `minimumScaleFactor` is allowed only on single-line metrics.

## Layout

A single scrolling column on the grouped ground, 16 pt horizontal gutter, 36 pt between top-level sections and 32 pt bottom padding. Inside a cell, content stacks on 12 or 16 pt spacing with 16 pt padding. Spacing runs on an 8 pt grid with 4 and 12 pt half steps (4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48).

Paired actions and side-by-side content use `AdaptiveStack`: horizontal when it fits, vertical when Dynamic Type or translation would truncate, so labels never clip. Every interactive element is at least 44 pt tall; full-width buttons are 52 pt.

**The One Decision Rule.** A cell states one thing and offers one way forward. The Weekly Check-In leads with the decision, then the week; evidence sits behind "Why?" rather than on the surface.

## Elevation & Depth

Content is flat. Cells separate from the ground by tone alone (white on #F2F2F7, #1C1C1E on black), with no stroke and no shadow in either appearance. Depth exists only for things that float above scrolling content: the toast, the rest-timer bar and the workout mini-player sit on regular material with a soft black shadow (12-15% opacity, 12-20 pt blur, 4-8 pt drop). Chart tooltips carry a smaller 6 pt shadow.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Cell Rule.** A content cell never casts a shadow or carries a border. If a surface needs a shadow, it must be floating over scrolling content on material.

## Shapes

Every corner is continuous (squircle). Cells are 14 pt; buttons and quick-action tiles 16 pt; quiet buttons and evidence wells 10 pt; tooltips 6 pt; the camera frame 28 pt. Chips, badges, progress tracks and macro bars are capsules; rings use round line caps. Icon badges are continuous rounded squares at 30% of their size, never circles. Hairlines are 0.5 pt.

## Components

### Buttons
Native, tinted and calm: one filled, the rest washed.
- **Shape:** gently rounded continuous rectangle (16 pt), full width by default.
- **Primary:** Action Green fill, white label (black in dark), body emphasized, 52 pt tall (44 pt compact), 24 pt side padding; optional leading SF Symbol at 8 pt gap. Disabled falls back to tertiary grey.
- **Press:** scales to 0.98 and dims to 92% on a 0.1 s strong ease-out; releases on the 0.28 s snappy curve.
- **Secondary:** Green Wash fill with Action Green Text label, same height and radius. Pairs to the left of a primary ("Keep current" / "Apply adjustment").
- **Quiet:** well fill, ink label, 10 pt radius, 44 pt minimum, pressed scale 0.97.
- **Inline action:** plain text in Action Green Text, secondary emphasized, 44 pt hit area.

### Chips
- **Style:** capsule, well fill, secondary-grey caption emphasized, 8 × 5 pt padding, optional small leading symbol. Muscle chips show three then "+n".

### Cards / Containers (cells)
- **Corner Style:** 14 pt continuous.
- **Background:** Cell White / #1C1C1E.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see The Flat Cell Rule).
- **Border:** none.
- **Internal Padding:** 16 pt.
- **Pressable cells:** scale to 0.985 and dim to 90% on press.

### Navigation
- **Tab bar:** the system `TabView` with SF Symbol tabs (`sun.max`, `dumbbell`, `fork.knife`, `chart.line.uptrend.xyaxis`, `person.crop.circle`), selection tinted Action Green Text.
- **Screen title:** a custom large title ("Today") with the long date in secondary grey beneath; the navigation bar is hidden on Today.

### Category Header (signature)
The first line of a cell: a tinted SF Symbol and sentence-case label in secondary emphasized, set in the category colour, with an optional trailing detail in secondary grey and a chevron when it navigates (44 pt hit area). Coach uses `scope`, Training `dumbbell`, Nutrition `fork.knife`. This is how a cell says where it belongs; nothing else on the cell is tinted for the category.

### Section Header
A sentence-case section title (20 pt bold, ink) between cells, with an optional trailing text action in Action Green Text, inset 4 pt.

### Metrics, rings and bars
- **Metric view:** caption label in secondary grey, value in a rounded metric style, optional unit in secondary, optional delta badge (arrow + signed percent, success or warning colour, grey when flat).
- **Progress ring:** well-coloured track, round-capped stroke in the category or macro colour; past 100% the overshoot wraps at 55% opacity so exceeding a target stays visible. Fills on appear with the 0.45 s gentle ease-out.
- **Macro bar:** coloured 8 pt dot + name, consumed / target in tabular figures, 6 pt capsule bar on a well track.
- **Nutrition summary:** calorie ring (136 pt, 13 pt stroke; 112 / 11 compact) beside the three macro bars.

### Icon badge
36 pt continuous rounded square, Green Wash fill with an Action Green Text glyph at 42% of the badge size; status variants swap to success or warning washes.

### Evidence list
A well-filled 10 pt panel of label / value rows separated by hairlines; values are tabular secondary emphasized. Revealed under "Why?" with a fade-and-drop on the 0.35 s smooth curve.

### Pro badge
A small Action Green capsule reading "PRO" in rounded heavy caption. It is a product tag, the one place capital letters appear, and it never sits above a heading as a label.

### Recommendation marker
The SF Symbol `scope` marks what Vector recommends (Coach header, "Use this", coach empty states). Never a sparkle.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every piece of content in a 14 pt Cell White cell on the grouped ground, padded 16 pt, separated by 36 pt.
- **Do** open a cell with a `CategoryHeader` in its category colour: Coach and Training green, Nutrition orange, Body indigo.
- **Do** keep to one filled Action Green button per view; make companions secondary (Green Wash) or inline text.
- **Do** set result numbers in SF Pro Rounded bold with tabular figures, and pair every status or macro colour with an icon, sign or label.
- **Do** use `AdaptiveStack` for paired buttons so they stack at large text sizes.
- **Do** press down on the 0.1 s ease-out and release on the 0.28 s snappy curve; collapse motion to a 0.15 s fade under Reduce Motion.
- **Do** keep bounce for rare moments (a PR, workout complete); completing a set stays crisp.

### Don't:
- **Don't** give content cells shadows, strokes or category-coloured fills.
- **Don't** use uppercase, letter-spacing or a small label above a heading (no eyebrows or kickers).
- **Don't** use a sparkle or magic glyph for recommendations; use `scope`.
- **Don't** introduce a second action colour or fill a button in a category colour.
- **Don't** add achievement badges, confetti, streak pressure or neon; don't use aggressive black-and-red.
- **Don't** use emoji; SF Symbols only.
