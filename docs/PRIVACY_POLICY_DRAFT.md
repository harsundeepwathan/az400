## 1. Summary
Kira is a personal finance tracker that works **on your iPhone**. We don't operate servers that receive your financial data, we don't sell data, and we don't use advertising or analytics SDKs.

## 2. Data processed and where
| Data | Source | Where it's processed | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transactions, accounts, balances, budgets, commitments, income | You (typed, imported, scanned, Shortcuts) | On your device only | Providing the app |
| Receipt images, statements, QR codes | Files/photos/camera you choose | On-device (Apple Vision, PDFKit). Images aren't stored after reading | Extracting transaction details |
| Subscription status | Apple App Store | On device via StoreKit | Unlocking paid features |
| Face ID / Touch ID | iOS | iOS only — Kira never receives biometric data | App lock |

We **never** collect online banking usernames, passwords or one-time codes.

## 3. Legal basis & consent
Processing happens on your device to provide the service you request. Features that would send data off the device (for example optional cloud sync, if offered later) will require separate, explicit consent before activation and can be withdrawn at any time.

## 4. Disclosure
We don't disclose your financial data to third parties, because it never reaches us. Apple processes payment and subscription data under Apple's own privacy policy.

## 5. Security
Data is stored in iOS's encrypted storage (Data Protection, "Complete" class) and is unreadable while your iPhone is locked. You can add Face ID / Touch ID / passcode protection. See the in-app "Privacy & data" page.

## 6. Retention & deletion
Data stays on your device until you delete it. **Settings › Your data › Delete all data** permanently removes Kira's records, pending captures, saved secrets and preferences. Deleting the app also deletes its data. Device backups (iCloud/Finder) follow your backup settings.

## 7. Your rights
You may access, correct, export (CSV/JSON — **Settings › Your data**) and delete your data at any time, regardless of subscription status. Contact us at [email] for any request relating to data we hold (normally none).

## 8. Children
