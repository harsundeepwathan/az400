# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users
People who train without a personal trainer, at every level: newcomers learning the basics, regular gym-goers (3–5 sessions a week in a commercial gym) who want to stop guessing, and experienced lifters who log RPE and follow programs. They use the app in two scenes: mid-workout between sets (phone in one hand, sweaty, glancing for a few seconds under gym lighting), and at home morning or evening logging food, weighing in and reviewing the week.

## Product Purpose
Vector is a digital fitness coach built around the user's own data: "Train. Eat. Progress. Vector figures out what to change next." It plans training, logs workouts and food, tracks body weight, and once a week makes one clear, explained decision (adjust calories, progress a lift, or change nothing). Success is a user who trusts the weekly decision and follows it.

## Positioning
A deterministic coaching engine decides; AI only explains. Every recommendation shows its evidence and confidence, and "Everything is on track, no changes" is a valid, frequent answer. Vector doesn't manufacture advice and doesn't claim to replace trainers.

## Operating Context
Between-set logging (complete a set, see feedback against the target, rest timer); Today screen as the daily answer; Weekly Coach Check-In; food logging by search, barcode and AI photo scan; weigh-ins, measurements and progress photos; Apple Watch logging; Pro subscription unlocks the coaching decisions.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Native iOS 17+ SwiftUI, iPhone only; widgets, Live Activities, Watch app.
- Free tier remains a complete tracker; the coaching decision, Apply Adjustment and coaching history are Pro.
- Safety boundary: not a doctor, physiotherapist or dietitian; no injury diagnosis; fixed pain guidance text.
- Must honour Dynamic Type, Dark Mode and Reduce Motion.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Vector (kept). Everything about the visual identity is open for replacement, including the icon and the monochrome "Ink" look.
- Visual direction (chosen by the owner, Oct 2026): the category standard, played straight at the highest craft level. The bar is Apple Fitness / Health, Strong / Hevy, MacroFactor and Whoop / Oura: native grouped layouts, colour that carries category meaning, big confident numbers, one decision at a time.
- Must not feel gamified (no badges, confetti, streak pressure, neon), clinical (cold spreadsheet or medical), or "bro" (aggressive black-and-red hardcore gym).

## Evidence on Hand
No real user testimonials, metrics, press or customer logos. Sample data in the web preview is synthetic.

## Product Principles
1. Decide, then explain: one decision at a time, with the evidence a click away.
2. "No change" is an answer, not an absence.
3. Accountability without guilt.
4. Free stays useful; Pro is the coach.
5. Never cross the safety boundary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Dynamic Type through accessibility sizes, VoiceOver labels on every control, 44 pt targets, Reduce Motion crossfades, WCAG AA contrast in both appearances.
